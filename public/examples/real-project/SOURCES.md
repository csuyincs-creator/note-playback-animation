# Real music example

Piece: Franz Schubert, Impromptu in A-flat major, D. 899 No. 4 (30-second excerpt).

- MusicXML, MIDI, sampled-grand MP3, and score PDF were downloaded from the public example files at [klavery.com](https://klavery.com/midi/schubert90no4.html).
- The source page describes the MIDI as an automatic transcription of a public-domain performance, and says the audio is that MIDI played on a sampled grand. This makes the supplied score, timeline, and audible performance the same excerpt; the MP3 is not the original acoustic recording.
- The source page says the composition is public domain and the MIDI may be reused. It does not show a separate license statement for the rendered MP3 or PDF. These files are bundled for this local evaluation example; check the source terms before redistributing the audio or score files.

The audio file runs about 33.4 seconds, including the sample's decay after the 30-second MIDI excerpt. The preview uses the audio duration so that the ending is not cut off.
